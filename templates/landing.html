Operation Lifecycle

How LogiStock Works

A streamlined 4-step workflow from vendor consignment arrival to final client dispatch.

01
Catalog Intake

Admins register unique SKUs, categories, locations, and safety reorder levels.

02
Receive Stock IN

Staff log incoming shipments at the loading bay with invoice notes and batch counts.

03
Dispatch OUT

Orders are deducted from stock with automatic overdraft prevention checks.

04
Live Insights

Dashboard aggregates inventory valuation and triggers alerts for depleted stock.
